Gina

Agentic wallet infrastructure. Gina is an AI agent equipped with its own wallet: describe what you want in plain language and Gina researches, trades, monitors and automates it for you, 24/7.

One wallet, every market. Gina transacts across 12+ blockchains, all of Polymarket's prediction markets, every Hyperliquid perpetual, and now stocks, commodities and indices. She writes and runs custom automations from a one-line prompt, and you can drive Gina from Claude Code, Codex and Cursor over MCP.
